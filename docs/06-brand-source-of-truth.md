## Official Position (use this)

**Market position:**  
Focused product-development partner between freelancers and enterprise consultancies — more structured than freelancers; more focused and flexible than large agencies.

**Recommended commercial position (Competitor Analysis):**  
Mernify builds scalable web, mobile and SaaS products for startups and growing businesses.

**Brand Guidelines positioning statement:**  
Modern software development partner for startups, growing businesses, agencies, and enterprises that need dependable web, mobile, and SaaS solutions without managing multiple technical vendors.

**Do not lead with:** “We are a MERN / MEAN company.”  
Stack expertise lives under technology — not as the brand promise.

**Gradients (sparingly):** Indigo→Cyan; Dark Navy→Slate Black  

**Type:** Space Grotesk (display/headings) · Inter (body/UI) · Fallbacks Arial/Helvetica/sans-serif  

**UI:** max width ~1200–1320px · button radius 8–12 · card radius 12–20 · soft shadows · cards selective  

**Logo:** combination mark; flat masters preferred; glow is mockup-only (Analysis). Clear space = height of “M”. Min digital full logo 120px; symbol 24px.
