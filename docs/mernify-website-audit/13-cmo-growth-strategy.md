## 13.9 Founder-Led LinkedIn Content Direction

**Objective:** Build a founder/CTO personal brand that attracts ideal clients through authentic content.

**Recommended content mix (monthly):**

| Content Type | Frequency | Example |
|-------------|-----------|---------|
| Project insight (from real work) | 4× | "This week we discovered that [technical insight]..." |
| Founder perspective (industry) | 4× | "Why SaaS founders should run a Discovery Sprint before writing any code" |
| Case study fragment | 2× | "How we reduced [client] deployment time by 40%..." |
| Behind the scenes | 2× | "How our dedicated pod model works..." |
| Opinion / hot take | 2× | "Why I refuse to commit to a project without a discovery phase" |

**Key rules:** Never post fake stats. Always share genuine insights. Engage with comments. Tag clients only with permission.
