( Why AI Integration )

AI Features That Are Evaluated, Not Just Deployed

The gap between an impressive AI prototype and a reliable production feature is evaluation: knowing when the model is wrong, what it costs at scale, and what happens when it fails in front of a customer. We treat AI features as software with measurable requirements — accuracy targets, latency budgets, and guardrails — not as a black box bolted onto your product.

Ship AI features with defined accuracy and latency targets, not just “it feels smart”

Avoid unpredictable model costs by architecting usage and caching correctly upfront

Keep sensitive data out of prompts sent to third-party model providers

Build an evaluation pipeline so quality doesn’t silently degrade after launch

( Capabilities )

What AI Integration Includes

Product-embedded assistants

Conversational features embedded directly into your existing product workflows, not a bolt-on chatbot in the corner.

Intelligent search & RAG

Retrieval-augmented pipelines that ground model responses in your own data, reducing hallucination and improving relevance.

Model provider integrations

Clean, swappable integration with providers like OpenAI, Anthropic, and open-weight models, avoiding hard lock-in.

Evaluation & guardrails

Automated evaluation suites and safety guardrails that catch regressions and bad outputs before your users do.

( Our Process )

How We Deliver AI Integration

Step 01

Use-Case & Data Discovery

We identify where AI actually creates measurable value in your product and what data is available to ground it.

Step 02

Model & Architecture Selection

We choose the right model provider, retrieval strategy, and cost profile for your specific use case and scale.

Step 03

Prototype, Evaluate & Guardrail

We build an evaluation set early and iterate against it, adding guardrails for safety, cost, and failure handling.

Step 04

Deploy & Continuously Tune

We ship to production with monitoring on quality and cost, then tune prompts and retrieval as real usage comes in.

Quick Answers

Maxwell Sterling

Co-Founder & CTO

We start with the business problem, not the model. If a simpler rules-based or search-based approach solves it more reliably and cheaply, we’ll tell you that instead of defaulting to AI.

We ground responses in your own data through retrieval-augmented generation, define an evaluation set to measure accuracy, and add guardrails that catch and handle low-confidence or out-of-scope responses.

We architect integrations to use provider APIs under data-use terms that keep your data out of model training, and can route sensitive workloads to private or self-hosted models where required.

We model expected usage and cost per interaction during architecture, implement caching and rate limiting, and monitor spend in production so costs don’t scale unpredictably with usage.

Yes — most of our AI work is embedding features into existing products, integrating with your current data model, authentication, and UI rather than shipping a separate standalone tool.

We define success metrics upfront — accuracy, latency, deflection rate, or task completion — and build a continuous evaluation pipeline so you can see quality trends, not just anecdotal feedback.
