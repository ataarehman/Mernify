## 14.1 Website as a Sales Tool

**Can a salesperson confidently send the Mernify URL to a prospect today?**

**Answer: Partially — with caveats.**

✅ What works:
- Clean, professional design builds first-impression credibility
- Hero messaging clearly communicates what Mernify does
- Service taxonomy is logical for a buyer
- Contact form is functional
- Case studies exist (though authorship needs verification)

❌ What doesn't work:
- No team names or photos — "who am I hiring?"
- Case study authorship unclear — "prove you built this"
- Contact form delivery uncertain — "did my message go anywhere?"
- No differentiation statement — "why Mernify over 50 other agencies?"
- No testimonials — no social validation
- No discovery call option — "I can't schedule a call directly"

## 14.7 Minimum Viable Sales Package

Before using the website as a sales tool in outreach, Mernify needs:

### Must Have (Week 1–2)
1. ✅ Calendly or booking link on contact page
2. ✅ Confirmed working contact form endpoint
3. ✅ Founder/team name and photo on About page
4. ✅ At least 1 verified case study with client permission
5. ✅ IP ownership statement on FAQ or Process page
6. ✅ Expected response time on contact page

### Should Have (Week 2–4)
1. At least 1 client testimonial
2. Engagement model overview (Discovery Sprint, MVP Program, Dedicated Pod)
3. "What happens after you contact us" section on contact page
4. 30-second intro video (optional but highly effective for outreach)

### Would Accelerate Growth (Month 2+)
1. 3 deep case studies with outcome metrics
2. Thought leadership articles demonstrating expertise
3. Industry-specific landing pages
4. Partner/agency page
